How a meal travels

  1. 1

    A donor posts food

    What is left, how much, where to pick it up and how long it stays fresh.

  2. 2

    A collector claims what they need

    They take only the servings they can use. The rest stays on the list for others.

  3. 3

    Pick up, or release

    Plans changed before pickup? The collector releases it and the servings go back on the list.

  4. 4

    The donor confirms

    Once the donor confirms the pickup, the claim is locked and counted as shared.

Two ways to take part

Pick a role when you sign up. You can switch any time from your dashboard.

🏪

Donor

Restaurants, caterers, bakeries, shops and households.

  • Post surplus food in under a minute
  • See who claimed it and their phone number
  • Confirm the pickup when the food is collected
  • Need food for someone? Switch to Collector

🚲

Collector

NGOs, volunteer groups and shelters.

  • Search fresh food by name, area or type
  • Claim only the servings you need
  • Release a claim if you can't make the pickup
  • Got more than you can use? Switch to Donor
